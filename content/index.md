Anyways, quick points on what I've worked on below, will only write a little here but I'm happy to discuss further (get my gmail by removing vowels from my name). My work is largely in LLMs (and some vision) consisting of:
- Explainable AI. Essentially trying to unpack data that gets embedded by foundation models, but there's different spins on what that could mean.
	- Studying the embedding spaces of deep neural networks (DNNs) to extract interpretable, explainable, and high-level information about our data. Plenty of statistical techniques here with relations to "geometric" questions about data. There's neat applications to understanding differences between AI-generated and real data. You can find the preprint [by clicking here](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2408.10437).
	- Sparse autoencoders and other areas of "mechanistic interpretability". Essentially, rather than use dimensionality reduction, do the opposite to try to achieve the similar means haha. It's considerably more expensive to do this, however. 
- RAG- and agentic-related applications. In particular been looking at handling knowledge graph (KG) modalities, both generating KGs through LLM-based knowledge discovery or using KGs for Q&A-related tasking. This involves lots of playing with different models and the rest of the AI stack. Been thinking about games with uncertainty quantification here...
