Turns out that Wayland is the default protocol for a display server on NixOS. This is nice for various reasons, but I always hear it's "the future" and everything is moving away from the X11 protocol.

Anyways, once you accept wayland then you can play around with other programs that make use of your display so that you can set up a [[Desktop Environment]].
